Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search for flatmates near KS Layout? expand_more

Once signed in, visit the Rooms section. You can filter roommate profiles and PG offers by DSU campus locations and budget ranges.

Are the confessions on KnotSpot actually anonymous? expand_more

Yes. The confessions engine doesn't store your user profile links when posting a confession. You are assigned a randomly generated code name (e.g. "Anonymous Tiger") to ensure total privacy.

Can students from all DSU branches join? expand_more

Absolutely! Whether you're in B.Tech CSE, MBA, B.Pharm, or design courses, you can log in and participate in discussions relevant to your campus and class.

Is this project officially run by DSU? expand_more

KnotSpot is a student-built community forum designed to connect peers. It is free and run independently for and by DSU college students.
